The Authors

Every version of every article on Finland's history carries a name. Loading…

How this is computed · The SQL

Everything here comes from revision, the metadata of all 862,076 versions (who, when, size, sha1, edit summary). scripts/build_authors.py streams it once into an in-memory DuckDB and writes small viz_authors_* tables back to MariaDB. Bots are accounts whose name ends in “bot”. Anonymous means logged out: an IP address, or since 2025 a temporary account (“~2025-…”). A class is the year of an editor's first version in this topic. Undone: a later revert by someone else restored the text from before the edit (sha1 match, spans up to 50 versions). “First words” are read live from the system-versioned article table with FOR SYSTEM_TIME AS OF.
