Wikipedia Through Time /Kielipeili /Framing check

Same event, two Wikipedias

MariaDB finds the paragraphs in which two language editions tell the same part of the story, as both stood on a date (FOR SYSTEM_TIME AS OF + VEC_DISTANCE_COSINE). The model reads the pairs that are told most differently and says what differs: the facts, or only the framing. Each quote is checked word for word against the stored revision; a failed quote goes back to the model once, and what still fails is not shown as evidence.
