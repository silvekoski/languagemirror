Typewriter

 

The opening section of a Wikipedia article, replayed through every stored version (FOR SYSTEM_TIME ALL). Typed words glow, deleted words strike through in red: the lead is where editors decide what matters most about a topic, and here you can watch that judgement form, get argued over and get reverted.
