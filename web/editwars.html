Edit Wars

Every time someone puts back an older text, somebody else's words disappear. We found all … reverts in 23 years of Finland's history on Finnish Wikipedia and sorted them: most are cleanup of vandalism within minutes, but … are edit wars, two registered editors undoing each other until one gives up. That is where the encyclopedia's knowledge was actually contested.

      Revert = a version whose text is byte-identical (sha1) to an earlier one but not to the previous one; the versions in between were undone.
  Edit war: both editors registered and each has reverted the other in this article.
  Dispute: a registered editor undone by another, one-way.
  Vandalism cleanup: the undone editor is anonymous, or was undone within 24 h with ≤ 10 edits in total.
  Bot: reverted by a bot. Self: editors undoing themselves.
