Every sentence of a Wikipedia article was written by someone, at some moment. Here each sentence is traced back to the edit that first wrote it, and the article is drawn as rock: every band is text from one year, deposited, buried under newer writing, sometimes eroded by later editors. Click the strata to dig: the reader shows the article exactly as it stood, read from MariaDB with FOR SYSTEM_TIME AS OF.
