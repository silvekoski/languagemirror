How the history of Finland filled up Wikipedia, 2003–2026

Every arc is a category under Suomen historia, sized by the wikitext its articles held at that moment, rebuilt from the metadata of all 862,076 versions in MariaDB. The rings are the tree's real annual rings: the size the whole tree had at the end of each year.

The SQL · how the tree is rebuilt for any date

The category graph is not a tree: 256 of the 1,475 categories have several parents. For the layout every category hangs under the parent on its shortest path from Suomen historia (breadth-first; ties go to the alphabetically first parent), and the panel lists its other parents. An article in several categories is split evenly between them, so the arcs add up to the true total. Categories are today's: Wikipedia's dumps carry no history for them.
