Heartbeat

Every one of the 862,076 versions of Finnish Wikipedia's history-of-Finland articles, February 2003 to October 2026, placed on the calendar and on the clock (Helsinki time). Knowledge doesn't change at a steady pace: it moves in pulses — election months, one-person clean-up campaigns, and more and more often bots that sweep through a thousand articles in a single day.

Every day since 2003

One row per year, one cell per day (Helsinki date). Hover a day for what was edited, click it to open the day.

Around the clock

Weekday × hour in Helsinki local time, summed over each era. Shares make eras of different size comparable.

What was being written about

Monthly edits in the biggest categories. Each ridge is labelled with the article that drove its highest month.

The SQL — precompute, the live day query, and FOR SYSTEM_TIME AS OF

Edits are versions in revision (metadata of every version, 2003-02-21 → 2026-10-01). Times are converted from UTC to Europe/Helsinki (EET/EEST). Registered = a logged-in account, anonymous = IP address or temporary account, bot = account name ending in “bot” (also “bot2”, “bot~fiwiki”, “(bot)”) or starting with “Bot”. Categories are the article's direct Finnish categories (article_category). A day counts as “driven” by one editor when they made at least half of its edits.
